03 / Ways to work together

Start where you are.

A specific question or a longer piece of work. These five engagements give us a place to start.

  1. Discovery Sprint

    Two to three weeks

    A half-day workshop, supplier and buyer interviews, and a written read-out. We examine where the brand stands and recommend the next step.

    • Deliverables
    • Diagnostic note
    • Recommended next step
    • Optional engagement scope

  2. Commercial Reset

    Six to eight weeksDefault engagement

    For brands ready to improve stock movement, distribution or channel returns. We rework pricing, partner terms and reorder plans for the next twelve to eighteen months.

    • Deliverables
    • Tiered pricing
    • Partner shortlist
    • Reorder mechanics
    • Buyer collateral

  3. Route-to-Market Review

    Four to six weeks

    For brands early in market or ready to enter another. We rework the route from buyer to brand, with a single-state focus as the default.

    • Deliverables
    • Route map
    • Distributor recommendation
    • Account targeting
    • Terms framework

  4. Buyer-Readiness Preparation

    One to three weeks

    For your next major buyer conversation, from national accounts to on-premise groups. We prepare the pitch around the criteria the buyer uses.

    • Deliverables
    • Pitch pack
    • Buyer-criteria mapping
    • Prep session
    • Optional in-room attendance

  5. Operator Retainer

    Three-month minimum, rolling

    Ongoing decision support, capped at twelve hours per month. Use it for pricing calls, distributor issues, supplier conversations and preparation before a meeting.

    • Channels
    • Phone
    • WhatsApp
    • Document review
    • On-demand meetings
